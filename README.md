## My Favorite Movie: *Pee Mak*

**Pee Mak** (2013) is a Thai horror-comedy-romance film based on the famous Thai folklore of **Mae Nak Phra Khanong**. After the war, Mak brings his soldier friends home, where the villagers are terrified of a ghost, and the friends begin to suspect that Mak's wife Nak is the ghost.

### Why I Like It

- It mixes **comedy, horror, and romance** perfectly
- The friendship of Mak's four soldier friends is hilarious
- The love story is sweet and sad at the same time

### Philippine Adaptation: *Pee Mak PH*

- Produced by **Viva Films**
- Directed by **Victor Villanueva**
- Mak is played by **Wilbert Ross** and Nak by **Bea Binene**
- Released in Philippine cinemas on **October 14, 2026**

### Original vs. Philippine Version
